One request for the whole row, never one per field: every route under /api/ shares a 60-per-minute-per-IP rate limit, so a field-at-a-time panel would spend the user's budget on typing. G54: Save, Cancel and Remove each carry their OWN hx-target and hx-swap. This panel is swapped-in content, so nothing outside the fragment is an ancestor of it and no target is inherited.
