Features

Turn optional parts of Shelf on or off. A feature that is off hides its tab, refuses its pages and actions, and pauses its background job. Its data is kept, and turning it back on shows everything again. To hide a tab without turning its feature off, use Navigation under Library.

{# Profiles: one plain form per preset, posting to /api/settings/profile. The matching profile's button is disabled and marked with a neutral accent border, never a status colour (G126); "Custom" shows when none matches. data-confirm comes from profile_rows, built from the same probe pass as the rows below, and only when applying would turn off something that stops for other people. #}

Apply a profile to set every feature below at once.
